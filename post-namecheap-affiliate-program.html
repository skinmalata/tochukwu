How the Namecheap Affiliate Program Works

The Namecheap affiliate program is straightforward: you promote Namecheap's products using a unique affiliate link, and when someone makes a purchase through that link, you earn a commission. Namecheap handles the tracking, reporting, and payments.

Products you can promote include domain registrations, web hosting, SSL certificates, email hosting, and more. Every product in Namecheap's catalog is eligible for affiliate commissions.

Namecheap offers a competitive commission structure based on the product type:

Domain Registrations 10% $0.90 on a $8.98 domain

The 15% commission on hosting is where the real money is. A single shared hosting sale at $58.56/year earns you $8.78. If you send 10 hosting sales per month, that's nearly $88 in passive income. Over a year, that scales nicely.

Domain commissions are lower per sale but much easier to generate—domains are a universal need for anyone building a website, and Namecheap's domain prices are competitive, which makes conversions easier.

Cookie Duration: 30 Days

Namecheap's affiliate cookie lasts 30 days. This means if someone clicks your affiliate link and doesn't buy immediately but returns within 30 days to make a purchase, you still earn the commission. This is generous compared to some programs that only offer 24-hour or 7-day cookies.

In practice, I see a significant number of conversions happen days or even weeks after the initial click. People often need time to research and decide before buying a domain or hosting plan. The 30-day window captures many of these delayed purchases.

Payment Methods and Threshold

Namecheap pays affiliates via PayPal. The minimum payout threshold is $50. Once your commissions reach $50, they're paid out monthly. If you don't hit $50 in a given month, the balance rolls over to the next month until you reach the threshold.

Payments are processed within 30 days after the month ends. For example, commissions earned in January are paid out by the end of February. This is standard in the affiliate industry.
