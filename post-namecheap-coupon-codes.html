How to Save More: Multi-Year Registration

One of the best ways to save money at Namecheap is to register or renew your domain for multiple years at once. Domain prices tend to increase over time. ICANN has approved increases to the .com wholesale price, and those costs get passed down to consumers.

By registering for 5 or 10 years upfront, you lock in the current rate and protect yourself against future price hikes. I registered several of my client domains for 10 years during a Namecheap sale, and those domains are now significantly cheaper than they would be if I renewed annually.

The math works like this: at $13.98/year, a 10-year renewal costs $139.80. If the price goes up by 7% annually (which is roughly the historical trend), you would pay approximately $186 over 10 years if you renewed each year. That is a savings of about $46, plus you never have to worry about forgetting to renew.

Free WHOIS Privacy: A Hidden Savings

Namecheap includes free WhoisGuard privacy protection on every domain. This might not seem like a big deal, but other registrars charge $8 to $12 per domain per year for WHOIS privacy. If you own 10 domains, that is $80 to $120 in annual savings just from the privacy protection.

WHOIS privacy hides your personal contact information from public WHOIS databases. Without it, your name, address, phone number, and email are visible to anyone who looks up your domain. This leads to spam calls, phishing attempts, and even domain hijacking attempts. Getting it for free with Namecheap is one of the best value propositions in the domain industry.
